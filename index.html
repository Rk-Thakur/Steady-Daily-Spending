FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Steady free?

Yes. Steady is free to download and use, with no ads.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. Steady is manual by design: you enter your balance and log spends yourself. It takes seconds, and it keeps you aware of your money.

What happens if I overspend?

Nothing dramatic. The extra is spread evenly over the days left until payday, so each day's number drops a little.

My income changes every week. Will it work?

Yes. Turn on the Paycheck Vault: big weeks fill it, and it pays you a steady amount each week so your number doesn't swing.

What if I lose my phone?

Your data is only on your phone, so create an encrypted backup file from Settings › Backup & export and keep it somewhere safe, like your cloud drive.
