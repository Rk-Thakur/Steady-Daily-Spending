FAQ

Common questions

How is my daily number worked out?

Steady takes the money you have, adds income logged this cycle, sets aside unpaid bills due before payday and your goal set-asides, then divides the rest by the days left until payday (including today). Tap the number on the Today screen to see every line.

Why did my number change?

The Today screen shows what changed since yesterday, for example "Down $5 from yesterday: you spent more". Tap the number to see "Since yesterday" with each reason.

I'm not getting reminders
  • Check Steady › Settings › Reminders. If it says Blocked, turn on notifications for Steady in your phone's Settings.
  • iPhone: Settings › Notifications › Steady › Allow Notifications. Also check Focus modes.
  • Android: Settings › Apps › Steady › Notifications, and allow "Alarms & reminders" so reminders arrive on time. Some phones also need battery optimisation turned off for Steady.
How do I back up my data or move to a new phone?

Go to Settings › Backup & export › Create backup file, choose a password, and save the .steady file somewhere safe (Files, a cloud drive, or email). On the new phone, install Steady and choose Restore from a backup file on the first screen.

I forgot my backup password

We can't recover it. Backups are encrypted with your password and we never have a copy, which is what keeps them private. Make a new backup with a password you'll remember.

I forgot my App lock PIN

If Face ID or fingerprint unlock is on, use that to get in, then change the PIN in Settings › App lock. If you still can't get in, email us. We can't see or reset your PIN, because it never leaves your phone, but we'll help you with the options.

How do I delete all my data?

Settings › Backup & export › Delete all my data. This erases everything Steady stored on this phone. Uninstalling the app does the same.

Does Steady connect to my bank?

No. Steady is manual by design, so no bank login is ever needed. See the privacy policy for exactly what stays on your phone.
